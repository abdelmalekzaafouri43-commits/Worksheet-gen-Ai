Present Perfect vs. Past Simple: Tense Mastery

Complete the exercises below paying close attention to time markers (since, for, already, yet, yesterday, in 2020).

EN
Mr. Zaafouri Abdelmalek

1 Section A: Choose the Correct Option

[5 Marks]

Circle the letter (A, B, or C) that best completes each sentence.

1. I ________ to London three times this year.

Answer: A (have been - unfinished time period)

2. Yesterday, she ________ her keys while walking in the park.

Answer: B (lost - specific past time "yesterday")

3. We haven't finished our science project ________.

Answer: C (yet - used with negative present perfect)

2 Section B: Verb Conjugation & Transformation

[5 Marks]

Put the verbs in brackets into the correct tense (Present Perfect or Past Simple).

1. Dr. Fleming ________________ (discover) penicillin in 1928.

Answer: discovered

2. Look at this! Someone ________________ (break) the classroom window.

Answer: has broken

3. How long ________________ (you / live) in your current city?

Answer: have you lived
